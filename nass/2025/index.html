11 charts reproduced from the published NASS statistical tables. Trends run 2022 to 2025; single-year profiles show 2025 only. Every chart carries the figures behind it as a table you can read or download.

Reading these figures

The trend charts here use the adult population, which is defined consistently from 2022 to 2025.

Trends are shown as counts rather than published percentages

Full detail is in the pipeline's validation report, which lists every difference found between editions.
