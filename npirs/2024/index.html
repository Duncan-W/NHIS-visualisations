Psychiatric admissions in Ireland, 2024

7 charts reproduced from Activities of Irish Psychiatric Units and Hospitals. Two run from 1965; the rest show 2024. Every chart carries the figures behind it as a table you can read or download.

Reading these figures

The two long series come from single tables that publish the full history, so no figures are joined across editions. Where a value is taken from an earlier edition it is named in the source line under that chart.

Diagnostic categories were renamed in 2023 and Health Regions replaced Community Healthcare Organisations in 2024, so neither diagnosis nor geography is shown as a trend.

Counts below five are suppressed in the source and appear as gaps, shown as published in the data tables.
